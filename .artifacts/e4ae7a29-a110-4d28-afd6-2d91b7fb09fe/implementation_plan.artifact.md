# Implementation Plan: Artifact Project Reference Document (Revision 1)

Revise the Artifact Project Reference Document to restore the project's original product philosophy, mission, and values while maintaining the improved hierarchical structure and engineering standards.

## Goal
Establish a single source of truth that is **Mission-First**, ensuring engineering standards support the core intent of connecting people through authentic human experiences.

## Proposed Structure & Content Restoration

1.  **Mission**: Restore to: **"Connect people through authentic human experiences, not identities."**
2.  **Vision**: Restore to: **"Building a Human Library of authentic human experiences."**
3.  **Core Values**: Restore the original 7 values (Thoughts over appearance, Authenticity over popularity, etc.).
4.  **Product Philosophy**: Restore focus on Voice over vanity, Reflection over impulsiveness, and Emotional safety.
5.  **Responsible Anonymity**: Maintain principle-based guidance from legal docs and privacy policy.
6.  **Listening Principles**: Restore "Listen Before You Publish" and "Listen Before You Respond" (LBYR).
7.  **Product Decision Framework**: Restore the 8-question evaluation framework for every feature.
8.  **Feature Evaluation Criteria**: Expand to include Mission alignment, Community health, and Sustainability alongside engineering risk.
9.  **UX Principles**: Restore Voice over vanity, No popularity systems, and No addictive design principles.
10. **Engineering Context**: Maintain MVVM, SSOT, and Repository ownership guidance.
11. **Architecture Evaluation**: Move engineering framework (Invariants, SSOT, Idempotency) here.
12. **Bug Investigation Methodology**: Maintain the structured sub-phase approach.
13. **Investigation Hierarchy**: Maintain the Evidence -> Reproduction -> Fix -> Verification flow.
14. **Evidence Standards**: Maintain the 4-level Confidence hierarchy.
15. **Investigation Report Format**: Maintain the standardized reporting template.
16. **AI Credit & Investigation Budget**: Refine to align with the analytical, mission-aligned communication style.
17. **Communication Style**: Restore: Analytical, Evidence-based, Honest about uncertainty, Root-cause focused, Trade-off aware, and Mission aligned.
18. **Artifact Terminology**: Maintain Artifact, Resonance, Sanctuary, Sigil, Draft.
19. **Workflow & Verification Standards**: Maintain pre-merge checklists and documentation quality standards.
20. **Manual Verification Guidelines**: Maintain standardized testing paths for production hardening.
