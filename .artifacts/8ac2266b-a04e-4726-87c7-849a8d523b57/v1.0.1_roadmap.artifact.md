# Artifact v1.0.1 Planning Roadmap (Refined)

This roadmap defines the post-launch strategy for Artifact v1.0.1 and beyond. It adopts an **evidence-driven** approach, prioritizing production stability and reliability metrics over speculative feature expansion.

## Executive Summary
Artifact v1.0.0 is currently in production with strong initial stability (99.9% crash-free). To avoid release churn, we will observe production behavior for **two weeks** before finalizing the v1.0.1 scope. This update focuses on foundational maintenance and localization, deferring all functional or architectural changes until they are supported by real-world usage data.

---

## Post-Launch Observation Strategy
*   **Week 1 (Monitor)**: Pure observation of Firebase telemetry, Play Vitals, and user feedback. Zero code changes unless a P0 incident occurs.
*   **Week 2 (Analyze)**: Correlate qualitative feedback with performance metrics. Finalize the "Maintenance" backlog.
*   **Target v1.0.1 Release**: T+14 to T+21 Days.

---

## Recommended v1.0.1 Scope: Maintenance & Polish
*High-confidence, low-risk improvements backed by current evidence.*

### [ ] **Foundational Localization**
- Migrate `FeedRecommendationReason.explanation` from hardcoded enums to `strings.xml`.
- Localize "Why this Artifact?" (menu label) and "I understand" (sheet action).
- Rationale: High value for non-English users; zero architectural risk.

### [ ] **UI/UX Polishing**
- Refine `RecommendationExplanationSheet` padding and typography for small-form-factor devices (e.g., 5" displays).
- Rationale: Identified during external validation as a minor layout friction point.

---

## Recommended v1.1.0 Scope: Incremental Evolution
*Reserved for architectural cleanup and minor functional refinements.*

- **AuthorizationService Refactor**: Consolidate moderation policy logic.
- **Architecture Consolidation**: Decommission redundant `ArtifactFeedCard.kt` wrapper.
- **Richer Context**: Support for "Theme-based" recommendation explanations.

---

## Deferred Until Supported by Production Evidence
*Speculative enhancements that require verified user behavior before implementation.*

- **Predictive Cache/Hydration**: Defer until Play Vitals confirm feed latency as a bottleneck.
- **AuraDock Low-Power Mode**: Defer until battery telemetry identifies GPU-heavy animations as a significant drain.
- **Sigil Rendering Optimization**: Defer until "Slow Frames" vitals confirm SVG redraws are impacting scroll performance.
- **New Recommendation Reasons**: Defer until user interaction data suggests existing reasons are insufficient or confusing.

---

## Product Health & Reliability KPIs
*Success is measured by the stability of the listening experience, not engagement counts.*

| KPI | Target | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Crash-free Sessions** | >99.95% | Maintain our highest stability tier. |
| **ANR Rate** | <0.47% | Stay below the standard Google Play threshold. |
| **Startup Time (P50)** | <2.5s | Ensure hydration remains fluid and immediate. |
| **Publishing Success** | >98% | Verify zero-data-loss for the "Ambient Upload" flow. |
| **Recording Success** | >99% | Confirm audio capture reliability across diverse hardware. |
| **Backup/Restore Success**| 100% | Ensure the "Encrypted Recovery" path is unbreakable. |

---

## Final Recommendation
Adopt the **2-week observation window**. Focus v1.0.1 exclusively on **Localization and Polish**. By letting production data drive the roadmap, we preserve the "Calm UX" mission and ensure that every release provides verified value to the community.
