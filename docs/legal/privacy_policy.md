# Privacy Policy for Artifact

Last Updated: July 4, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Artifact is a voice-first social platform built on the principles of **Responsible Anonymity**. We prioritize your privacy and the authenticity of your emotional expression.

## 2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

### a. Authentication & Identity
- **Firebase Authentication**: We use Google Sign-In to verify your identity. We collect your name and email address to manage your account and secure your data.
- **Anonymous Identity**: We generate a unique, non-identifiable nickname and sigil for your public presence. Your real name and email are **never** shared with other users.

### b. Audio Data & Voice Recordings
- **Artifacts**: When you publish an "Artifact", your voice recording is uploaded to our secure cloud storage.
- **Drafts**: Audio drafts are stored locally on your device and may be synced to your private storage for persistence.
- **Processing**: Audio may be processed locally and/or by backend services for transcription, safety analysis, abuse detection, and content moderation, depending on the enabled features. We use these services to help protect your privacy before you publish.

### c. Metadata & Social Interaction
- **Cloud Firestore**: We store your profile data (avatar config, anonymous name), artifact metadata (titles, timestamps), and follow relationships.
- **Comments**: We store comments you make on artifacts.

### d. Technical & Security Data
- **Firebase App Check**: We use Play Integrity to prevent unauthorized access and abuse.
- **Firebase Crashlytics & Performance Monitoring**: We collect technical data to identify and fix bugs and optimize app performance.
- **Analytics**: If enabled, we collect anonymized usage data to improve the Artifact experience.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
- **Infrastructure**: All cloud data is stored securely using Google Firebase services.
- **Local Storage**: We use encrypted local storage for sensitive session data and offline caching of your artifacts.
- **Security Measures**: We implement industry-standard encryption and access controls to protect your data.

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion
- **Drafts**: Local drafts remain on your device until you delete them or clear app data.
- **Published Artifacts**: Artifacts remain accessible until you choose to delete them.
- **Account Deletion**: You can permanently delete your account via the App Settings. Upon deletion, we dissolve your presence and delete all associated audio recordings, transcripts, and profile data from our servers within 30 days.
- **Cached Data**: Local caches are cleared upon logout or account deletion.

## 5. Safety & Moderation
- **Reporting & Blocking**: You can report harmful content or block users. We process these reports to maintain community safety.
- **Responsible Anonymity**: While you are anonymous to others, we maintain accountability to prevent harassment and illegal activity.

## 6. Your Rights and Controls
- **Access & Export**: You can request an export of your emotional archive (audio and metadata) via the App Settings.
- **Rectification**: You can update your anonymous profile (avatar, sigil) at any time.

## 7. Contact Us
For privacy inquiries or to exercise your data rights, contact: **privacy@myartifact.app**
