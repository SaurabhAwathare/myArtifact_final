How to Delete Your Account

You have full control over your emotional journey on Artifact, including the right to dissolve your presence and delete your data.

Steps to Delete Account

  1. Open the Artifact app on your Android device.
  2. Tap on your Presence (profile icon) to open your hearth.
  3. Tap the Settings icon (gear) in the top right corner.
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the Settings menu.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Confirm your choice. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

What Happens Next?

  • Immediate Effect: Your presence is dissolved, and you will be logged out. Other users will no longer be able to find your profile or hear your artifacts.
  • Data Deletion: All your audio recordings, transcripts, profile metadata, and follow relationships are queued for permanent deletion from our servers.
  • Timeline: Data is typically removed within 30 days of the request.

Need Help?

If you no longer have access to the app or need assistance, please contact us at privacy@myartifact.app from the email address associated with your account.
