Account Deletion Request

We respect your privacy and your right to be forgotten. You can delete your Artifact account and all associated data through the following methods.

1. Authoritative In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you still have the Artifact application installed, the most secure and immediate way to delete your account is from within the app:

  1. Open the Artifact app.
  2. Navigate to Settings (via the Sigil icon in the top right of the feed).
  3. Scroll to the Data section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm deletion.

This process immediately signs you out, clears local data, and triggers an automated backend process to remove your profile and recordings from our servers.

2. External Deletion Request

If you have already uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you may request a manual account deletion by contacting us.

Verification Required To protect your account from unauthorized deletion, we must verify your ownership. Please send the request from the same Google email address used to create your Artifact account.

To initiate a request, please email our support team at:

Request Deletion via Email

What data will be deleted?

  • Your platform identity (email and name linked to your account).
  • Your anonymous profile (Sigil, generated name, bio).
  • All your voice recordings (Artifacts and Drafts) stored in our cloud storage.
  • Your relationship history (users you followed or were followed by).

What data may be retained?

Reflections (comments) you shared in the community will be anonymized to preserve conversation integrity but will no longer be linked to your identity. Certain safety reports or security logs may be retained for a limited period as required by law or to ensure community safety.

Once processed, you will receive a confirmation email. Manual requests are typically handled within 7 business days.
