Account Deletion

You have full control over your emotional journey on Artifact. If you wish to permanently delete your account and associated data, you can do so here.

Important Information

  • Permanence: Account deletion is irreversible. Your profile, artifacts, and social connections will be dissolved.
  • Anonymized Content: To preserve the integrity of ongoing conversations, comments you have shared on other users' Artifacts may remain visible, but will be permanently anonymized—your identity will be removed from them.
  • Data Cleanup: Once you trigger deletion, our automated systems begin purging your personal information, audio recordings, and metadata (including reaction and play history).
  • Timeline: The deletion process begins immediately. Some backend cleanup operations may complete asynchronously.
