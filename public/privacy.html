Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

Artifact is a voice-first social platform built on the principles of Responsible Anonymity. We prioritize the authenticity of your emotional expression and the security of your presence.

1. Responsible Anonymity

Artifact uses authentication to maintain accountability and protect the community. Public participation is based on an anonymous Artifact identity rather than your real-world identity. This allows you to share authentic experiences without turning Artifact into an identity-driven social network.

2. Information We Collect

a. Account & Authentication Information

We use Google Sign-In to verify your identity. Artifact receives your name and email address from Google. This Platform Identity is stored in private database collections and is used for account management, security, and re-authentication. It is not shared with other Creators.

b. Anonymous Profile Information

Your Public Artifact Identity consists of a generated Anonymous Name and a unique Sigil. This identity is separate from your real-world name and is what other Artifact Creators see.

c. Artifacts and Voice Recordings

When you publish an "Artifact", your voice recording is uploaded to our secure cloud storage. We store metadata such as the title, chosen emotion, and amplitude data used to render the visual waveform of your voice.

d. Interaction Information

We store data about how you interact with the Human Library, including when you Resonate with an Artifact, follow another Creator, or share a Reflection (comment).

e. Technical Information

We collect technical data to ensure service integrity, including hashed device fingerprints and security tokens used by Firebase App Check and Play Integrity to prevent unauthorized access and abuse.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain the Artifact service.
  • To secure your account and prevent abuse.
  • To generate contextually relevant Reflection Prompts using AI assistance.
  • To deliver notifications when others resonate with your presence.

AI Assistance Transparency

When generating Reflection Prompts, Artifact may send limited, non-identifying data to our AI service provider (Google Vertex AI). This data includes your current chosen emotion, the time of day, and a scrubbed summary of your session context. Your voice recordings and raw transcripts are never sent to the AI service.

4. Data Storage and Protection

All cloud data is stored securely using Google Firebase services. We use industry-standard access controls to protect your data. Local data, including drafts and settings, is stored securely on your device.

Data Portability & Deletion You can export your reflections or initiate account deletion from within the Artifact application settings. For users who no longer have the app installed, an external deletion request path is available.

5. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your Artifact account from the Settings section within the Artifact Android application. When you initiate account deletion:

  • Initiation: The process begins immediately upon your confirmation in the app.
  • Cascading Cleanup: Our systems asynchronously remove your Platform Identity, profile data, and associated voice recordings from cloud storage.
  • Anonymization: Reflections (comments) you have shared remain visible to preserve conversation integrity but are permanently disconnected from your identity.
  • Retained Information: Certain data, such as safety reports or security logs, may be retained for a limited period to prevent fraud, ensure community safety, or meet legal obligations.

6. Child Safety Standards

Artifact is committed to maintaining a sanctuary for authentic expression while ensuring the safety of all users, particularly children. We have a zero-tolerance policy for content that exploits or endangers minors.

a. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Artifact is dedicated to protecting minors from exploitation and abuse. We strictly prohibit the use of our platform for any activity that endangers children, including the sharing of child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, or solicitation of minors.

b. Zero Tolerance for Prohibited Content

The following is strictly prohibited on Artifact:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Content depicting or promoting the sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any conduct that endangers a minor.
  • Inappropriate sexual content involving minors.

c. Reporting a Child Safety Concern

If you encounter an Artifact or Reflection (comment) that violates these standards, please report it immediately inside the application:

  1. Tap the Reporting option on the relevant Artifact or Reflection.
  2. Select the most appropriate reason (e.g., Sexual Content or Other).
  3. Provide optional details in the description field to help our team investigate.
  4. Submit the report.

Your report is anonymous to the community. Locally, the reported content will be hidden from your view immediately.

d. Moderation and Enforcement

Reports are recorded in our secure moderation system for review. Artifact uses a combination of community-driven suppression and human review:

  • Automatic Suppression: Content that receives multiple community reports is automatically suppressed from recommendation feeds pending review.
  • Administrative Review: Authorized moderators review reports through the Artifact moderation dashboard.
  • Enforcement Action: Violations of these standards will result in the permanent removal of violating content and the immediate termination of the associated Artifact account.

e. Responsible Anonymity and Accountability

Under our principle of Responsible Anonymity, while Artifact Creators appear anonymous to the community, Artifact maintains the internal Platform Identity necessary to enforce safety standards. Anonymity on Artifact is not a shield for abuse; we maintain account-level accountability to ensure the safety of our ecosystem.

f. Cooperation With Legal Authorities

Artifact will take appropriate action when prohibited child-safety material or conduct is identified. We will cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement and comply with applicable legal obligations to report illegal activity to the appropriate regional and national authorities.

7. Artifact Deletion

Individual Artifacts can be deleted within the application. Deleting an Artifact removes the audio recording and its primary metadata. Associated interactions are cleaned up through automated backend processes.

8. Third-Party Services

Artifact uses Google Firebase and Google Play Services for authentication, storage, infrastructure, and security. We do not share your Platform Identity or voice recordings with third-party advertisers.

9. Contact Us

For questions about your data or to exercise your privacy rights, please contact: supportartifact@gmail.com
