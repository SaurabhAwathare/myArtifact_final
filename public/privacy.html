Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Artifact is a voice-first social platform built on the principles of Responsible Anonymity. We prioritize your privacy and the authenticity of your emotional expression.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

a. Authentication & Identity

Firebase Authentication: We use Google Sign-In to verify your identity. We collect your name and email address to manage your account and secure your data. Your real name and email are never shared with other users.

b. Audio Data & Voice Recordings

Artifacts: When you publish an "Artifact", your voice recording is uploaded to our secure cloud storage. Audio may be processed for transcription and safety analysis.

c. Metadata & Social Interaction

We store profile data (anonymous nickname, sigil), artifact metadata, and follow relationships in Cloud Firestore.

3. Data Storage and Security

All cloud data is stored securely using Google Firebase services. We implementation industry-standard encryption and access controls to protect your data.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can permanently delete your account via the App Settings. Upon deletion, we dissolve your presence and delete all associated audio recordings, transcripts, and profile data from our servers within 30 days. See our Account Deletion Instructions for more details.

5. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or to exercise your data rights, contact: privacy@myartifact.app
